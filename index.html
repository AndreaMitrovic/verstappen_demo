Max Verstappen


Max Verstappen may have narrowly missed his fifth title in 2025 by just two points to Lando Norris, but he remains a true champion in the hearts of racing fans.
Despite the razor-thin loss, the brilliant Dutch driver earned immense respect across the paddock.

It's no surprise given his legendary 2023 season, where he completely rewrote F1 history by racking up an incredible 575 points, 19 wins, and 21 podiums.

Major Records Broken

Consecutive Wins: Won 10 Grands Prix in a row from Miami to Italy, breaking Sebastian Vettel's previous record.

Points Total: Set an all-time F1 record with 575 points in a single season, claiming 92.7% of all available points.

Single-Country Wins: Became the first driver to win three times in the same country in a single season (Miami GP, United States GP in Austin, and Las Vegas GP).

Helped Red Bull Racing win 21 of 22 races (95.4%), securing the Constructors' Championship.

Verstappen has been making history from the very start, capturing his first-ever Grand Prix victory at the 2016 Spanish GP when he was only 18 years and 227 days old.
